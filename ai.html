    Rupeyantra AI: Your Smart Finance Assistant

    Calculate EMIs, plan SIPs, compare loans and manage your budget with Rupeyantra AI. Get clear financial explanations and explore useful money-planning tools in one place.

    Rupeyantra Voice

    What Is Rupeyantra AI?

    Rupeyantra AI is a free finance assistant for people in India. Ask about EMIs, SIPs, loans, budgets and savings in Hindi, Hinglish or English and get a clear explanation, with the calculation shown whenever numbers are involved.

    Which Calculators Are Available?

    When you ask in the chat, these are worked out by a built-in calculator: EMI, SIP, lumpsum and FD maturity, CAGR, simple interest, GST, inflation, budget and savings goals, EMI comparison and SIP scenarios.

    The plus menu also opens 22 interactive calculators with charts and CSV download: EMI (with prepayment and a year-by-year schedule), SIP, step-up SIP, lumpsum, FD, RD, PPF, SWP, CAGR, compound interest, simple interest, inflation, retirement planner, goal planner, budget planner, loan comparison, loan eligibility, loan tenure, credit card payoff, emergency fund, GST and a money doubler.

    How Does Rupeyantra AI Work?

    Your question is sent to the Rupeyantra server. Calculations such as EMI or SIP are run by the built-in calculator and checked by a second, independent calculation before the numbers are shown; the AI only explains the result. A "Verified by calculator" note appears only when those checks pass.

    Answers about interest rates, taxes or government schemes are given only when they can be confirmed from official Indian government or regulator websites. If they cannot be confirmed, the assistant says so instead of guessing.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Are the results guaranteed?

    No. Results are estimates based on the numbers you enter. Returns, interest rates and inflation are assumptions and can change, so please check current rates with your bank or an official source. Rupeyantra AI gives general information, not financial advice.

    Is my chat saved?

    Your chats are saved only in your own browser so you can reopen them from the history panel. Deleting them there removes them from this browser only. Your messages are sent to our server and to the AI providers it uses to write replies, so deleting local history does not delete anything held elsewhere. Never share OTPs, PINs, passwords or card numbers.

    Can I upload files?

    Yes. You can attach up to 3 files to a message: photos, PDFs up to 2 MB, or text files such as TXT, CSV, TSV, JSON, MD and LOG. Photos are resized in your browser before they are sent. For text files only the first 8,000 characters are used. Figures read from a document should always be checked against the original.

    Can it create files?

    Yes. Use the plus menu, choose Create file, and pick a report, CSV sheet, simple web page, text file or JSON. Please check any figures in the file before you use it.
