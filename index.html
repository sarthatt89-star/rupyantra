About Rupeyantra AI & FAQ

What is Rupeyantra AI?

Rupeyantra AI is a free finance assistant for people in India. You can ask about EMIs, SIPs, loans, budgets and savings in plain language and get clear answers along with calculators and charts.

Which calculators are available?

EMI (with prepayment and a year-by-year schedule), SIP, step-up SIP, lumpsum, FD, RD, PPF, SWP, CAGR, compound and simple interest, inflation, retirement planner, goal planner, budget planner, loan comparison, loan eligibility, loan tenure, credit card payoff, emergency fund, GST and a money doubler.

Are the results guaranteed?

No. Calculator results are estimates based on the numbers you enter. Returns, interest rates and inflation are assumptions and can change, so please check current rates with your bank or an official source. This is general information, not financial advice.

Is my chat saved?

Your recent chat is saved only in your own browser so you can continue later, and the New chat button deletes it. Your messages are sent to our server to generate replies, so please never share OTPs, PINs, passwords or card numbers.

Can it create files?

Yes. Use the plus menu and choose Create file to get a report, a CSV sheet, a simple web page, a text file or JSON. Please check any figures in the file before you use it.

General information only, not financial advice.
